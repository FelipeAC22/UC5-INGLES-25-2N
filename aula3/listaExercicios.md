# Exercícios de Inglês – Verb To Be / Do, Does, Did / Simple Present

## Nível Básico

### 1. Complete com am / is / are

a) I am a student.  
b) She is my friend.  
c) They are happy.  
d) We in the classroom.  
e) He is a teacher.  

---

### 2. Complete com do / does

a) Do you like pizza?  
b) Does she play soccer?  
c) Do they study English?  
d) Does he work here?  
e) Do we have class today?  

---

### 3. Complete com o verbo correto (Simple Present)

a) I study (study) every day.  
b) She likes (like) chocolate.  
c) They play (play) soccer.  
d) He works (work) a lot.  
e) We live (live) in Brazil.  

---

### 4. Transforme em negativa

a) I am tired. → i am not tired  
b) She is my sister. → She is not my sister  
c) They are at home. → They are not at home
d) You like coffee. → You dont like coffee 
e) He plays football. → He doesnt play football  

---

## Nível Médio

### 5. Transforme em interrogativa

a) You are happy. → Are you happy?
b) She is at school. → Is she at school?
c) They are friends. → Are they friends?
d) You like music. → Do you like music? 
e) He works a lot. → Does he work a lot?

---

### 6. Complete com do / does / did

a) Did you go to the party yesterday?  
b) Does she like chocolate?  
c) Do they finish the homework?  
d) Does he study every day?  
e) Did you watch the movie last night?  

---

### 7. Complete com was / were

a) I was at home yesterday.  
b) They were very tired.  
c) She was at the party.  
d) We were late.  
e) He was sick last week.  

---

### 8. Coloque na forma negativa (Simple Present)

a) She likes pizza. → She doesnt like pizza
b) He works here. → He doesnt work here  
c) They study English. → They dont study english
d) You play soccer. → You dont play soccer
e) We watch TV. We doesnt watch TV 

---

### 9. Corrija as frases

aa) She do her homework every day. → She does her homework every day
b) They is very happy. → They are very happy
c) He don't like pizza. → He doesn't like pizza
d) I is a teacher. → I am a teacher
e) We does not understand. → We dont understand

## Nível Difícil

### 14. Traduza

a) Você estuda todos os dias. → You study every day
b) Ela trabalha muito. → She works a lot
c) Eles gostam de música. → They like music
d) Você joga futebol? → Do you play soccer?
e) Nós não assistimos TV. → We dont watch TV

### 15. Crie frases

a) (she / like / coffee) → She likes coffee
b) (they / study / English) → They study English
c) (he / not / work / here) → He does not work here
d) (you / play / soccer) → You play soccer
e) (we / watch / TV / every day) → We watch TV every day
